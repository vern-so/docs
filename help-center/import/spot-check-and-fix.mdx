The import agent gets most things right, but it isn't perfect. Use the sheet to
check a few values, and the workbook chat to fix a column it interpreted wrong
or to transform something after the fact.

## Work in the sheet

Open the company and click a sheet on the **Data** tab. A sheet behaves like a
normal spreadsheet:

- **Click** a cell to select it, **double-click** to edit.
- **Click and drag** to select a range. **Shift+click** column headers to
  select several columns; right-click to insert or delete.
- **Undo** (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) works per sheet: it undoes your last change on the sheet you are in.
- Invalid cells are tinted red. Where Vern has a likely fix, the cell offers
  **AI-suggested corrections** to pick from.

[Calculated](/help-center/templates/calculated-columns) (**ƒx**) and
[extracted](/help-center/templates/extracted-columns) (**✦**) columns are
read-only — change their formula or extraction on the template instead.

### Filter

Click the **Filters** icon in the toolbar. Its tabs narrow the sheet to what you
need to look at:

| Tab | Does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | Filter records by validation status — valid or invalid. |
| **Columns** | Show or hide columns in the table. |
| **Quick** | Filter rows by cell content. |
| **Complete** | Filter records by data completeness. |
| **Duplicates** | Find potential duplicate records. |

Click **Apply Filters**; **Clear all** resets them.

## Bulk-fix with chat

For anything bigger than a one-off edit, open the workbook chat.

Reference columns by typing `@` and picking from the list — it works across
sheets (`@Contacts.Phone`). Double-clicking a column header inserts it for you.
Some examples:

- `Fix the format of @Phone Number`
- `Combine @First Name and @Last Name into @Full Name`
- `Extract emergency contact phone numbers from @Notes into @Emergency Phone`
- `Check every source record came across — are any contacts missing?`

How chat works:

- It runs on the same migration agent as the import, so it knows the source
  files, the recipe and your templates.
- Changes arrive as a card showing what will change. **Approve** it to apply
  it, or skip it. Nothing is written until you approve.
- You can **Stop** it mid-answer if it's heading the wrong way.
- Conversations are saved, and you can keep more than one.

## Templates do the heavy lifting

Chat and the import agent use your template's column descriptions and rules to
know what good data looks like. If either keeps misinterpreting a column, fix
the column's [description](/help-center/templates/columns#description).
