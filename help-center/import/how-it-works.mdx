---
title: How import works
description: The migration agent that maps your customer's data into your templates — you watch, steer and approve before anything is written.
---

Most of the work of onboarding a customer is converting their messy export into
your clean schema. Vern's **migration agent** does that for you. It reads the
files, works out how they map onto your templates, writes a script (the
**recipe**) to do it, previews the result, and asks you whenever something
needs a decision. Nothing is written to the workbook until you click **Run
import**.

## Start an import

Open the company and click **Import** (or **Re-import** from the workbook's
**⋯** menu). Then on the upload screen:

1. **Add the files.** Click or drop files into the upload area.
   - Accepted: **CSV, Excel** (`.xlsx`, `.xls`, `.xlsm`, `.xltx`, `.xltm`),
     **SQL dumps** (`.sql`, `.bak`), **XML** and **ZIP** archives of any of these.
   - Up to 5 GB per file, 20 GB per upload, and 400 files per import.
   - Files already in the workbook from an earlier import can be brought in too —
     turn on **Include previous data**.
2. **Add instructions (optional).** **Instructions for this migration** is for
   anything specific to this run — *"ignore archived jobs"*, *"phone numbers are
   all Australian"*.
3. Click **Start import**.

If the company's [source](/help-center/sources/sources) has an API connector,
you can switch on **Extract via {Source}** instead of uploading — enter the
customer's credentials once and click **Extract** (or **Extract & upload** to
combine both).

<Note>
  PDF, Word and image files (scanned documents) are **temporarily
  unavailable**. Uploading one shows *"Document extraction (PDF, image and
  presentation files) is temporarily unavailable. CSV and Excel files are
  supported."* Documents inside a ZIP are skipped and listed as skipped.
</Note>

If you upload a file whose name matches one already in the workbook, Vern asks
whether to **Skip** it, **Upload anyway**, or **Replace** the existing one.

## Watch the agent work

The import opens as a console with four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | The agent's progress, its questions, and the changes it proposes. |
| **Recipe** | The script it's writing to transform the data (shown as **SQL** if your account uses [SQL recipes](/help-center/settings/recipe-format)). |
| **Uploads** | The source files, so you can see what it's reading. |
| **Preview** | What each sheet will look like, from a dry run on the data, with invalid cells marked. |

Along the way the agent may stop and ask:

- **Questions** — how to read an ambiguous column, what to do with partial
  records, which of two date formats is right. Pick an answer or type your own,
  then click **Continue**.
- **Template updates** — when the data has something your templates can't hold,
  it proposes the change. Click **Apply updates** to accept, or carry on
  without. Turn on **Template lock** on the company to stop it proposing these.
- **Conflicts** — when the data contradicts a template rule, it shows both sides
  and asks which wins. Click **Apply** on your choice.

For things it can look up itself — what a source system's status code means, an
industry abbreviation — the agent searches the web rather than asking you.

### Steer it

There's one message box at the bottom:

- Type a change and click **Apply edit** — *"split @Name into first and last"*,
  *"drop rows where status is test"*. The recipe and preview update.
- Switch on **Ask** to ask a read-only question — *"what happens to rows with no
  email?"* — without changing anything.
- On any of your messages, click **Undo from here** to roll the import back to that
  point.
- The **⋯** menu picks the **Model** — **Speed**, or **Thinking** for hard
  migrations — and has **Clear chat**.

## Run it

When the preview looks right, click **Run import**. The agent runs the recipe
over all the data and writes the rows to the workbook. When it finishes you'll
see *Successfully imported N rows*, and the sheets show every value checked
against your template rules: invalid cells are tinted red (often with an
AI-suggested correction to pick from), and warnings from AI rules are tinted
orange.

[Calculated](/help-center/templates/calculated-columns) and
[extracted](/help-center/templates/extracted-columns) columns are filled in as
part of the run.

## Stop or cancel

- **Stop** ends what the agent is doing right now. It stops on the server, not
  just in your browser.
- **Cancel import** abandons the run and returns you to the upload screen,
  keeping your files and answers.

Only one import can run for a workbook at a time.

## Imports get faster

Every import produces a **recipe** — the portable logic for *"this source →
your templates"* — and keeps the customer-specific details separate. On the
next import from the same source system, the agent starts from the best
earlier recipe instead of from scratch, so the second customer you onboard from
a system is much faster than the first. To make one recipe the default for a
source, use **Publish & bind import recipe** on the [source](/help-center/sources/sources).

What the agent learns about the customer goes into the company's **Knowledge**
tab, and what it learns about the source system into the source's knowledge —
both are read on every later run. Turn off **Memory** on the company to keep a
test run from saving anything.

## After import

If something needs fixing — a value didn't normalize, a rule was misread —
[spot-check and fix](/help-center/import/spot-check-and-fix) in the sheet.
