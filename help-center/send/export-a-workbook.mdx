---
title: Export a workbook
description: Get cleaned data out of Vern — download it as CSV, or write it straight into your app's API with a writer.
---

Once a workbook looks right, click **Export** in the workbook header (or on the
company page). The dialog has two tabs:

| Tab | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **CSV** | You want a file — to hand off, load manually, or keep as a backup. |
| **Writer** | You want the rows written straight into your app through its API. |

The **Writer** tab only appears once you've [built a writer](/help-center/send/write-to-an-api).

<Note>
  Exporting is **Admin-only**, because it takes a customer's whole dataset out
  of Vern. Members who try get a permission error. See [Team](/help-center/settings/team).
</Note>

## CSV

1. Under **Sheets**, tick the sheets to include (**Select All** / **Deselect
   All**). Each shows its record count, and how many are invalid.
2. Click **Export**.

Vern prepares the file in the background (*Preparing export…*) and the download
starts on its own when it's ready.

- **One sheet** downloads as a CSV named like
  `acme-workbook-employees-2026-11-09.csv`.
- **Several sheets** download as a ZIP, `acme-workbook_sheets.zip`, with one CSV
  per sheet.

The headers are your template's column names, one row per record. **Every row
is included**, invalid ones too — what you export is what’s in the sheet, so
fix the invalid rows first if you only want clean data.

Finished exports stay on the dialog's **Recent** tab for 24 hours, so you
can **Download** them again; after that they expire.

## Writer

A writer pushes the workbook into your app's API — no endpoint to build on your
side.

1. Choose the **Writer** tab and pick a writer.
2. Check the **Base URL** and credentials. They're prefilled from the writer;
   edits here apply to this run only.
3. Check the mapping: each sheet is matched to one of the writer's templates,
   and any sheet the writer can't write is flagged.
4. Click **Write to API**.

Progress streams live. Failures are grouped by the API's response — for example
`HTTP 422: email is invalid` — so you can see exactly which rows were rejected
and why. Fix the data and click **Write again**, or click **Fix with the build
agent** to have the writer's agent correct the mapping.

[Build a writer →](/help-center/send/write-to-an-api)

## From your backend

To pull cleaned data from your own code instead, use the Migration API's
