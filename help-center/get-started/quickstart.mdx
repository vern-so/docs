This walks you from an empty account to a customer's data sitting in your app.

<Tip>
  In a hurry? Go to [Home](/help-center/home/overview), attach the customer's
  export and say *"build the migration for this customer"*. The agent does every
  step below once you approve its plan.
</Tip>

## Before you start

- A Vern account at [app.vern.so](https://app.vern.so).
- A sample export from a real (or fake) customer of yours — CSV, Excel, a SQL
  dump, XML, or a ZIP of those.

## 1. Create your templates

A template defines what your data should look like — the columns you need, and
the rules each one has to pass.

1. Go to [Templates](https://app.vern.so/templates) and click **New**.
2. Choose **Create with AI** to have an AI draft your templates from a schema,
   spreadsheet or API docs, **Upload LinkML file** if you already have a
   `vern_templates.yaml`, or **Create from scratch**.
3. Review the columns. Tighten anything the AI got wrong.

[Generate templates with AI →](/help-center/get-started/generate-templates)

## 2. Create a company

A company represents one customer you're onboarding. It holds their workbook
(one sheet per template), their files, and what Vern learns about them.

1. Go to [Companies](https://app.vern.so/companies) and click **Create**.
2. Enter the customer's **Name**, and optionally the **Source** system they're
   moving off.
3. Pick the **Templates** that become sheets in their workbook, then click
   **Create Company**.

[Learn more about creating a company →](/help-center/onboard/create-a-company)

## 3. Import the data

Vern takes you straight to the import page.

1. Drop in the customer's files and click **Start import**.
2. The migration agent reads them, maps them onto your templates and shows a
   **Preview**. Answer any questions it asks.
3. When the preview looks right, click **Run import**.

[Learn how import works →](/help-center/import/how-it-works)

## 4. Check it

Open a sheet. Invalid cells are tinted red. Fix single cells by hand, or ask the
workbook chat to fix a whole column.

[Spot-check and fix →](/help-center/import/spot-check-and-fix)

## 5. Send it to your app

Click **Export** in the workbook header.

- **CSV** — download one sheet, or a ZIP of several.
- **Writer** — write the rows straight into your app's API.

[Export a workbook →](/help-center/send/export-a-workbook)
