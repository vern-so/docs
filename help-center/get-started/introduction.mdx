Vern helps SaaS teams move customer data from a customer's old system into yours, fast.

Start on **[Home](/help-center/home/overview)**: describe the migration, attach the customer's files or link your GitHub repo, and the Vern agent plans the whole job — the company, sources, templates, knowledge, writers and any scheduled sync — then builds it across Vern once you approve. Or do each step yourself: define your templates, create a company for the customer, import their files with the migration agent, check the result, and send it to your app.
