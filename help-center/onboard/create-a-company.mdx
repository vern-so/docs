title: Create a company
description: Set up a company for one customer you're onboarding — its workbook, knowledge and migration settings.
---

A company holds everything for one customer you're onboarding: their workbook
(one sheet per template), the files you import for them, what Vern has learned
about them, and their migration settings.

## Create one

1. Go to [Companies](https://app.vern.so/companies) and click **Create**.
2. Fill in the **Create Company** dialog:
   - **Name** — typically your customer's name.
   - **External ID** — filled in from the name; change it to the id your own
     system uses for this customer. It's how the [Migration API](/migration-api/create-a-migration)
     finds the company again.
   - **Source** — the system the customer is moving off, if you know it. Pick
     one from the list or **+ Add source**. See [Sources](/help-center/sources/sources).
   - **Templates** — the templates that become sheets in their workbook.
3. Click **Create Company**. Vern builds the workbook and takes you straight to
   the [import](/help-center/import/how-it-works) page so you can add their files.

You can also ask [Home](/help-center/home/overview) to create the company for you
— attach the customer's export and it sets up the company, workbook and import
in one plan.

## The company page

The header shows the company's **Source**, its **External ID** and its
**Migration ID** (the id the Migration API uses); click either id to copy it.
The **⋯** menu has **Edit** and **Delete**.

The main button follows the migration's state — **Import**, **Import N files**,
**Continue import**, **View import** or **Re-import**. **Export** appears once
the workbook has records.

Three tabs:

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Data** | The workbook's sheets, with record and invalid counts. Click one to open it. |
| **Knowledge** | Notes about this customer that the import agent reads before every run — their conventions, preferences and exceptions. The agent adds to it as it learns, and you can edit it. |
| **Settings** | Two switches for this migration (below). |

### Settings

- **Memory** — *Saves what it learns for next time.* Vern keeps the import
  recipe, source notes and this customer's preferences, and reuses them on the
  next import. Turn it off for a one-off or test migration: the import still
  runs and rows are still written, but nothing it learns is kept. You can only
  turn it off after the migration has completed a run.
- **Template lock** — *Stops Vern changing the data model.* No new columns and
  no changes to validation rules, not even for a single import. The agent maps
  what fits and tells you what it couldn't carry.

## Archive a workbook

To put a finished workbook out of the way without deleting it, choose
**Archive** from the workbook's **⋯** menu. Archived workbooks are listed under
**Archive** on the Companies page, where you can unarchive or delete them.
