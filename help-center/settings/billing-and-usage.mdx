Open the user menu (bottom left) and choose **Billing and Usage** or **Storage**.

## Billing and Usage

The page shows your current plan, what it includes, and this month's usage.

- **Credit plans** include a monthly allowance of **Vern Credits**. Credits are
  consumed **per source cell** an import reads, and usage beyond the allowance
  is billed at $0.02 per credit. The page charts usage and breaks it down per
  migration, and shows when monthly usage resets.
- **Pay-as-you-go** plans bill per 1,000 workbook rows imported.

Click **Manage Plan** to change your plan or payment details in Stripe. Only
the teammate on the Stripe subscription or a company Admin can manage billing.

Runs that cost credits — imports, scouts, lists, workflows and writer runs,
including those started by the [Home agent](/help-center/home/overview) or the
[Migration API](/migration-api/introduction) — need an active plan.

## Storage

**Storage** shows how much space uploaded files use across all your companies,
against your account's limit, with a per-company breakdown of files and size.

- Near the limit you'll see **Approaching your storage limit**.
- At the limit, **Storage limit reached** — new uploads are paused until space
  is freed.

To free space, delete companies or uploaded files you no longer need. A
[retention policy](/help-center/settings/data-retention) can do this for you
automatically.
