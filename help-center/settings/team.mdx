Vern accounts are shared across your team. Add anyone who needs to manage customers, run imports, or configure templates.

## Invite a teammate

Open the user menu (bottom left) and choose **Invite team**. Click **Add User**,
enter their **Name**, **Email** and **Role**, then open the **Add User** menu and
choose how they get in:

| Option | What they receive |
|---|---|
| **Send invite link** | An email to confirm their address and join. |
| **Send temporary password** | An email with a one-time Vern link that signs them straight in; they set their own password when they first log in. |

Either way they set their own password — Vern never emails one. Once they're
in, they can see your companies, templates, sources and workbooks, subject to
their role.

<Note>
  A **temporary password** link expires after **72 hours** and can be used
  **once** — it's built so that email link scanners can't use it up. If an
  invite lapses, open the person's row menu and click **Resend Invitation**.
</Note>

## Roles

Two roles. The difference is about **account-level control and data leaving
Vern** — not about who can do day-to-day import work.

<ResponseField name="Admin" type="role">
  Everything a Member can do, plus:

  - Add and remove teammates, and change roles
  - Manage billing (as can the teammate on the Stripe subscription)
  - Configure [data retention](/help-center/settings/data-retention) and the [recipe format](/help-center/settings/recipe-format)
  - Create, edit and delete [API keys](/help-center/settings/api-keys)
  - **Export data** — downloading sheets as CSV or a ZIP
  - Build, edit and run [writers](/help-center/send/write-to-an-api) and [source builders](/help-center/sources/source-builder), and manage stored source credentials
  - Connect and disconnect [GitHub](/help-center/settings/integrations) and [Slack](/help-center/settings/slack)
</ResponseField>

<ResponseField name="Member" type="role">
  Full access to the day-to-day work: open companies, upload files, run imports,
  review and fix flagged rows, edit templates.

  Members **cannot** export data, manage API keys, build writers or source
  connectors, or configure credentials.
  Attempting one of those returns a permission error rather than failing
  silently.
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
  Export is Admin-only deliberately: it materialises a customer's entire dataset
  into a file that leaves Vern, so it belongs with billing and API keys rather
  than with import work. If teammates who used to export now can't, this is why —
  make them Admins, or have an Admin run the export.
</Warning>

Admins change someone's role straight from the **Role** column.

## Remove a teammate

Open their row's menu, click **Delete** and confirm. Their Vern login is removed
and their session ends immediately. Anything they created stays in your account
— work isn't tied to one person.

## Your own account

Choose **Account** in the user menu to change your name and avatar. **Dark
Mode** is a switch in the same menu.
